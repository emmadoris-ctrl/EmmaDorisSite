Emma Doris Morcos: RAVE

Based in Stockholm, Emma is a design student with experience in music and visuals.

This will be evident in a new design project, The Emma Rave, where she and another designer, Emma Lette, invite you to experience a project where music influences visuals through creative coding.

The Emma rave: An instillation where the music you create controls the art, a visual spectacle using the different parameters of sound. Use the piano and your voice to create your own soundscape.

The different paremeters of sound and what they influence: pitch becomes colour, high or low notes make the brush float up or sink down, volume sets brush size and speed, and how long a note is held makes the brush curl.

About

Design

A voice becomes a set of brushes. The voice is split into up to four frequency peaks, so a single voice paints with several brushes at once, and each MIDI key gets a brush of its own.

The paint then flows, drips, blurs and slowly fades, like a lava lamp, over a background that cycles through colours. We designed a system of rules and parameters rather than a single image, so the performer and the sound complete the artwork live.
